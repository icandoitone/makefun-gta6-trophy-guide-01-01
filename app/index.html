The road to the platinum

PowerPyx rates the platinum 4 out of 10 and about 200 hours: one playthrough, no online trophies, no difficulty settings. The work falls into three steps.

  1. Step 1 · Play the story

    Five chapters, about 60 to 80 hours. Take the optional missions as they appear, scan vehicles and study animals on the way. Do Lucia’s balcony meditation in Chapter 1 — it may be the one missable spot.

    60–80 hours

  2. Step 2 · The 100% checklist

    After the credits, open Progress, Stats, 100% Completion. It asks for about 60% of true completion: 200 of 277 vehicles, 100 of 255 animals, 50 of 66 weapons, 70 of 80 challenges.

    About 100 hours

  3. Step 3 · Strangers & cleanup

    Finish the 8 Strangers & Freaks strands and the side-character finales, then sweep up what is left. Nothing here is part of the 100% checklist, so it needs its own pass.

    About 10 hours

Three things before you start. There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so keep trophy runs cheat-free. One trophy, Positive Mental Attitude, may be missable in Chapter 1.

About this guide

Every trophy shows its official name, description and grade exactly as PlayStation publishes them. The guides are written in our own words for this entry, and each one names its sources — a guide with no source gets no credit for its specifics, so every claim you can act on carries one. Where no guide has pinned down an unlock yet at launch, the guide says so plainly instead of guessing.

The list itself comes from the public trophy trackers: Exophase, PSNProfiles and TrueTrophies, as published on 18 November 2026, cross-checked against PowerPyx, GTABase and GTA Zone. The game has no online trophies, so the Online filter is empty on purpose.

This app is fully offline. It loads nothing from the network, it stores your checklist only in this browser, and it shows no Rockstar logo or official artwork.
